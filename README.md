# Job Boards in Finland

A comprehensive list of places to look for jobs in Finland, organized by category.

## General Job Boards

- [TE-palvelut (Employment and Economic Development Offices)](https://www.te-palvelut.fi/en/) - Official Finnish public employment service
- [Mol.fi](https://www.mol.fi) - The Finnish Job Market (formerly part of TE-services)
- [Oikotie Työpaikat](https://www.oikotie.fi/tyopaikat) - One of Finland's largest job portals
- [Monster.fi](https://www.monster.fi/) - International job board with Finnish listings
- [Duunitori](https://duunitori.fi/) - Popular Finnish job search engine
- [Indeed Finland](https://fi.indeed.com/) - International job search platform
- [Jobly](https://jobly.fi/) - Finnish job aggregator

## Tips for Job Seekers

- Many Finnish companies post jobs in both Finnish and English, but learning basic Finnish can significantly improve your chances
- Register with TE-palvelut (Employment Office) for access to their job database and support services
- Networking is important in Finland - attend industry events and use LinkedIn actively
- Check company career pages directly, as not all positions are posted on job boards
- Consider reaching out to recruitment agencies specializing in your field
